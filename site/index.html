First-principles conductance of a 1D Hf₂Se₉–VSe₃ heterojunction working

Members: Seongbin Park · Young Woo Choi (advisor)

A single Hf₂Se₉ molecule bridges two metallic VSe₃ chains, forming a 1D molecular junction grown inside a carbon nanotube. The project computes the junction's conductance from first principles and asks one physical question: does current flow through the molecule, and through which orbital? The transport evidence says yes — removing the molecule eliminates the conductance entirely — and the present frontier is correcting the molecular level positions (DFT underestimates the gap) so the resonance sits where it physically should.

Idea

VSe₃ forms a 1D chain; among the trichalcogenides its coordination had no experimental report, and here it is identified as trigonal prismatic (TP) and is metallic — a natural 1D electrode. Encapsulating a Hf₂Se₉ molecule between two such chains makes a metal–molecule–metal junction. Because the molecule's HOMO–LUMO gap is tiny at the DFT level (0.023 eV, vdW-DF2), its frontier resonance sits almost on the Fermi level and the computed transmission is anomalously high. Whether the transport picture is resonant or non-resonant tunnelling therefore hinges on getting the molecular level alignment right.

Method

Chain and junction relaxations, band structures, and PDOS are computed with DFT in SIESTA (VASP supplies the B3LYP reference), with vdW-DF2 as the production functional — PBE+D2 was anomalously unstable for the Hf₂Se₉ relaxation and was dropped (02. Hf₂Se₉ stability). Zero-bias transport is computed with NEGF (TranSIESTA / TBtrans) on the ~220-atom device (electrode k = 100, broadening η = 0.005 eV), giving the transmission $T(E)$, PDOS, and transmission eigenchannels. The molecular levels are then corrected with DFT+Σ: vacuum-molecule orbital shifts $\Delta_i=\varepsilon_i^{\text{B3LYP}}-\varepsilon_i^{\text{vdW-DF2}}$ (built from same-code differences through a common PBE reference) enter the device Hamiltonian as the projector $\Delta H = S\,C\,\mathrm{diag}(\Delta_i)\,C^{\dagger}S$ on the Hf₂Se₉ block — implemented through TBtrans's built-in δH, no source patch.

Status

Structure is settled (vdW-DF2 relaxation; 60° staggered registry favoured by 43.3 meV). Transport is converged: scattering-region length is converged, k-grid and η converged, and the three-way comparison pristine 0.70 / heterojunction 0.35 / disconnected 0 for $T(E_F)$ proves the molecule is the sole conduction bridge (a single dominant eigenchannel). The DFT+Σ molecular-level correction is applied (occupied levels shift down, virtual up; isolated-molecule gap opens 0.02 → 0.75 eV): on the 9-cell device the molecular resonance moves off $E_F$, and $T(E_F)$ drops to 0.0032. The remaining analysis is the interface charge-transfer $\Delta\rho$ — the δH correction enters at the TBtrans post-processing step, so the zero-bias $T(E)$ suffices and no finite-bias chain is needed.
