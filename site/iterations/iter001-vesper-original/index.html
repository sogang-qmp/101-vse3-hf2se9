VSe₃ TP/TAP structure & Hf-doped molecule junction in CNTs archived

Members: yw-choi, bychanlee, nangman98 · created 2026-03-16

Idea

Transition-metal trichalcogenides (MX₃) form 1D chains in either a TP (trigonal prismatic) or TAP (trigonal anti-prismatic) coordination. Te-based MX₃ are reported as TAP and Nb as TP, but VSe₃ had no experimental report. The goal is to identify the structure of a VSe₃ single chain synthesized inside a carbon nanotube (CNT) by atomic-resolution STEM — confirming the TP structure for the first time — and to compare TP vs TAP stability with DFT.

Core questions (as stated in the README)

  1. For a VSe₃ single chain, which is more stable, TP or TAP?
  2. How does TP/TAP preference differ between Se and Te (referencing existing JACS Te data)?
  3. Is an Hf-doped molecule structure stabilized inside a CNT?

Extension

  • Hf doping → observation of a 1D molecule junction + structure proposal.
  • Ultimate target: a 1D metal superlattice structure (concept proposal).

Method / scope

DFT with SIESTA (NAO basis), VASP (PAW), and Quantum ESPRESSO, comparing exchange–correlation functionals (PBE, vdW-DF2, PBE+D2, PBE+D3(BJ), HSE06) and van der Waals corrections. The work spans VSe₃ TP/TAP chains, the Hf₂Se₉ molecule/chain, the VSe₃–Hf₂Se₉ heterostructure, and (planned) CNT-confined and transport calculations.

Pages

See Status for the current state, and the result pages below for each completed analysis. Key results so far: TP is more stable than TAP for VSe₃; the isolated Hf₂Se₉ molecule/chain relaxes to an over-expanded Hf–Hf distance; the bare VSe₃–Hf₂Se₉ separation curve has no minimum, but H termination of the VSe₃ interface Se restores a minimum near d ≈ 3.5 Å.
