References

Every external reference used on this site: full citation + link, what it contains, and where we used it. Grouped by topic.

A. DFT+Σ molecular-level correction

  1. J. B. Neaton, M. S. Hybertsen, S. G. Louie, Phys. Rev. Lett. 97, 216405 (2006). DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.97.216405
    Origin of the correction. GW shows the metal-surface image charge renormalizes the molecular HOMO–LUMO gap relative to gas phase — an interface polarization effect semi-local DFT misses. Used in: physical basis of our level correction (Methods: DFT+Σ); direction-of-gap-change check in Results: level correction.
  2. S. Y. Quek, L. Venkataraman, H. J. Choi, S. G. Louie, M. S. Hybertsen, J. B. Neaton, Nano Lett. 7, 3477 (2007). DOI 10.1021/nl072058i
    The DFT+Σ transport recipe. Gas-phase gap correction + image-charge scissor for benzenediamine/Au; bare DFT overestimates the conductance. Used in: direct template for our per-orbital scissor $\Delta_i$ (Methods: DFT+Σ, Section B of level correction).
  3. M. Strange, C. Rostgaard, H. Häkkinen, K. S. Thygesen, Phys. Rev. B 83, 115108 (2011). DOI 10.1103/PhysRevB.83.115108
    Validation against full GW. Self-consistent GW transport (no scissor approximation) for thiol-/amine-linked benzene/Au; justifies approximating $\Sigma$ as a static, orbital-diagonal shift. Used in: justification of our static level-1 δH (Methods: DFT+Σ).
  4. F. Montes, H. Vázquez, Appl. Sci. 11, 802 (2021). DOI 10.3390/app11020802
    Original SIESTA/TranSIESTA implementation — the self-energy correction added to the Hamiltonian as an orbital-dependent operator (benzenediamine/Au; DFT overestimates conductance 5–13×, DFT+Σ brings it to 1.0–1.8× of experiment). Full text verified 2026-07-02 (Eq. (1): isolated-molecule and MPSH projector constructions both sanctioned); PDF in repo references/. Used in: implementation precedent and the expected size of the conductance drop (Section C discussion of level correction).
  5. Fallaque et al., J. Phys.: Condens. Matter 34, 435901 (2022). DOI 10.1088/1361-648X/ac89c4
    Efficient TranSIESTA re-implementation with an infinite-reflection image charge, benchmarked on acene/Au (full text verified). Its workflow — extract $H_\text{mol}, S_\text{mol}$ from .TSHS (sisl) → MPSH — is the procedure we adapted; we replaced the MPSH projector with isolated-molecule orbitals because our molecule is covalently fused into the chain. Used in: workflow template + MPSH-vs-isolated-projector decision (Methods: DFT+Σ, Log 2026-07-02).

B. Method / software documentation

  1. M. Paulsson, M. Brandbyge, Phys. Rev. B 76, 115117 (2007). DOI 10.1103/PhysRevB.76.115117
    Transmission eigenchannels from nonequilibrium Green's functions — the eigenchannel construction (spectral function → $\tau_n$ decomposition) our eigenchannel figures follow. Used in: eigenchannel sections of transmission and level correction.
  2. SIESTA 5.2 TBtrans reference — docs.siesta-project.org
    The δH mechanism: $H \leftarrow H + \delta H$ via TBT.dH, no source patch. Used in: how ΔH enters the device Green function (Methods: DFT+Σ).
  3. sisl write_delta — sisl.io.tbtrans.delta · issue #129
    The level-1 (E- and k-independent) deltaH.dH.nc writer/reader matching TBT.dH. Used in: building our ΔH file (Methods: DFT+Σ).
