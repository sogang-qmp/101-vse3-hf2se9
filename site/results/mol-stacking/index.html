09. Hf₂Se₉ molecular stacking

2026-07-29 · Calculations: fixed-geometry distance scans and ongoing relaxations. Experimental TEM: Dr. Yangjin Lee (KIST).

Goal

Determine the terminal Se–Se separation $d$ and hydrogen arrangement for an Hf₂Se₉ dimer (2 molecules) and trimer (3 molecules).

Experimental basis (TEM)

Method

  • Electronic structure: vdW-DF2 (van der Waals density functional, LMKLL), 500 Ry real-space mesh cutoff, Γ point, and a vacuum cell.
  • Distance scan: fixed-geometry single-point energies at $d$ = 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 3.6, 4.0, and 4.5 Å for the dimer; the trimer omits 3.6 Å.
  • Hydrogen arrangements: 3 H atoms at the mid-gap plane with triangular radii 0.82, 1.97, and 3.0 Å. The labels bond, gap, and tilt are project labels for these three starting geometries.

Result

The hydrogen-terminated gap geometry has its minimum at $d$ = 3.5 Å for both the dimer and trimer. The nearest measured spacing is 3.6 Å; for the dimer, the 3.6 Å point is 0.135 eV above the 3.5 Å minimum. In the unterminated scan, the minimum occurs at the lower scan bound, $d$ = 2.5 Å.

Relaxation in progress

Both structures remain unconverged against the maximum-force tolerance of 0.02 eV/Å. The current calculations use conjugate-gradient relaxation with a maximum displacement of 0.01 Å. Live force values are listed on Status.
