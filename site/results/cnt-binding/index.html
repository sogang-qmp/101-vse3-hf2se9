10. CNT encapsulation — binding energy

2026-07-24 · Source: 03-calc/phase-c/01_binding (siesta.stdout).

Goal

Binding of the VSe₃ wire and the Hf₂Se₉ molecule inside a (9,9) CNT (diameter ≈ 12.2 Å):

$$E_\mathrm{bind} = E(\mathrm{X@CNT}) - E(\mathrm{X}) - E(\mathrm{CNT})$$

Method

vdW-DF2 (LMKLL), MeshCutoff 500 Ry; the three terms share the identical cell and CNT atoms (VSe₃: 30 × 30 × 12.4629 Å, 4 f.u. : 5 CNT rings, k = (1,1,8); Hf₂Se₉: z = 19.6761 Å, k = (1,1,6)). The complex is relaxed with the CNT fixed (guest free, 0.02 eV/Å); the two references are single-point.

Result

Both guests bind (negative $E_\mathrm{bind}$); the Hf₂Se₉ value is preliminary — its relaxation is still running.
