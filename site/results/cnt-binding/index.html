10. CNT encapsulation binding

2026-07-29 · Source: vdW-DF2 total energies for X@CNT, isolated X, and the empty (9,9) CNT.

Goal

Determine the binding energy of a VSe₃ wire or Hf₂Se₉ molecule, denoted X, inside a (9,9) carbon nanotube (CNT; diameter ≈ 12.2 Å):

$$E_\mathrm{bind} = E(\mathrm{X@CNT}) - E(\mathrm{X}) - E(\mathrm{CNT})$$

Method

All three terms use vdW-DF2 (LMKLL), a 500 Ry real-space mesh cutoff, the same cell, and the same k-point grid. X@CNT and the empty-CNT reference use identical carbon coordinates. The CNT is fixed while X is relaxed to a maximum-force tolerance of 0.02 eV/Å. The VSe₃ cell is 30 × 30 × 12.4629 Å with a 1×1×8 grid; the Hf₂Se₉ cell has $c$ = 19.6761 Å with a 1×1×6 grid.

Result

VSe₃ encapsulation is exothermic: $E_\mathrm{bind}$ = −4.854 eV per cell = −1.213 eV per VSe₃ formula unit. The Hf₂Se₉@CNT relaxation is not converged, so its final binding energy is not yet reported.
