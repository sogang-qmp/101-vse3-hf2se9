10. CNT encapsulation — binding energy

2026-07-24 · Source: 03-calc/phase-c/01_binding (siesta.stdout total energies; structures from _scripts/build_at_cnt_v2.py). VSe₃@CNT complete; Hf₂Se₉@CNT relaxation in progress.

Goal

Quantify how strongly the two 1D building blocks — the VSe₃ wire and the Hf₂Se₉ molecule — bind inside a (9,9) carbon nanotube (diameter ≈ 12.2 Å), the encapsulation geometry of the experimental samples. The binding energy is

$$E_\mathrm{bind} = E(\mathrm{X@CNT}) - E(\mathrm{X}) - E(\mathrm{CNT}), \qquad \mathrm{X} = \mathrm{VSe_3\ wire,\ Hf_2Se_9\ molecule},$$

so that a negative value means the guest is bound inside the tube.

Method

All three terms are computed with vdW-DF2 (LMKLL), MeshCutoff 500 Ry, and a k-grid of (1,1,8) for the VSe₃ system, in the identical cell (30 × 30 × 12.4629 Å, tube axis z) with the identical CNT atoms, so cell- and k-grid errors cancel between the terms. The commensurate cell holds 4 VSe₃ formula units against 5 CNT rings (+1.34% axial strain on the CNT; the wire is unstrained). The complex is relaxed with the 180 CNT carbons fixed and the wire free (force tolerance 0.02 eV/Å); the two reference terms (wire in the same cell, empty CNT) are single-point energies. The Hf₂Se₉ system uses the same protocol in a 19.6761 Å cell (8 CNT units, k = (1,1,6)), which keeps the periodic images of the single molecule 12.7 Å apart.

Result — VSe₃@CNT

The relaxed wire stays centred in the tube and binds with $E_\mathrm{bind}$ = −4.85 eV per cell (4 formula units), i.e. −1.21 eV per VSe₃ unit or −0.39 eV per Å of tube.
