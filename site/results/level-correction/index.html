Molecular-level correction (DFT+Σ) and length convergence

2026-06-26 · current frontier · A longer scattering region confirms the result; B3LYP orbital shifts computed as the input to the DFT+Σ level correction

Goal

Two things before the level-corrected transport: (1) confirm the conductance is converged once the scattering region (the central device between the two semi-infinite VSe₃ electrodes) is made longer, and (2) compute the B3LYP→vdW-DF2 molecular-orbital energy shifts $\Delta_i$ that feed the DFT+Σ correction.

A. Scattering-region length convergence

The 60° heterojunction was relaxed and its transport recomputed with the scattering region extended from 3 to 9 VSe₃ unit cells per electrode (cell-fixed relaxation, vdW-DF2; transport at electrode k-point grid 100, η = 0.005 eV). Geometry, molecular PDOS, and transmission all converge.

Relaxation — interface geometry converges by 7 unit cells

The interface bond lengths and the molecule–chain gap stop changing by 7 cells: the molecule–chain (Se···VSe₃) gap moves 50 → 8 → 1 mÅ and is stable at 9.

Molecular PDOS is length-independent

The molecular Hf₂Se₉ PDOS is nearly identical across 3 / 5 / 7 / 9 unit cells — the peak positions do not move, confirming length-independent molecular levels.

Eigenchannel vs energy — resonant tunnelling

Real-space $|\psi|^2$ isosurface of the transmitting eigenchannel at four energies. At $E_F$ the channel is delocalised across the molecule (on-resonance); moving away in energy it becomes localised / decays at the molecule (off-resonance) — the signature of resonant tunnelling through the molecular level.

B. B3LYP molecular-level correction (DFT+Σ) — inputs

The semi-local functional (vdW-DF2) underestimates the molecular gap, so the transmission resonance is mis-placed at $E_F$. The correction is a DFT+Σ level alignment: each molecular orbital is shifted by $\Delta_i = \varepsilon_i^{\text{B3LYP}} - \varepsilon_i^{\text{vdW-DF2}}$ and added to the device Hamiltonian as

$\displaystyle \Delta H = S\,C\,\mathrm{diag}(\Delta_i)\,C^{\dagger}\,S,$

with $S$ the overlap and $C$ the molecular-orbital coefficients. From the vacuum molecule (vdW-DF2 in SIESTA vs B3LYP in VASP, vacuum-level aligned) the gap opens 0.02 → 0.75 eV with a strongly asymmetric shift — occupied levels drop ≈ 1 eV ($\Delta_\text{HOMO} = -1.01$ eV) but virtual levels only ≈ 0.1–0.3 eV ($\Delta_\text{LUMO} = -0.29$ eV). The device transport uses the same vdW-DF2, so the correction functional is consistent.

It is outside the transport window and is not used in the correction.

Discussion / next

  • No SIESTA source patch needed — the shift applies through TBtrans's built-in δH term (TBT.dH); the workflow and pitfalls are documented in the repo.
  • Next deliverable: apply $\Delta H$ and recompute the corrected $T(E)$ — the implementation and plots are not done yet.
  • Open caveat: the absolute $\Delta_i$ carry a common offset (~−0.65 eV) between the two codes (vdW-DF2 in SIESTA vs B3LYP in VASP); a rigorous $\Delta_i$ needs both functionals from the same code (B3LYP and the device functional in VASP).
