What we have established, by topic

In short: a single Hf₂Se₉ molecule bridges two metallic VSe₃ chains and is the sole conduction path — removing it eliminates the conductance. Conduction is resonant because the molecular frontier levels sit on $E_F$, which is exactly why the level alignment now needs correcting. The findings, one page each:
