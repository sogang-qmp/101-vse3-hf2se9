Settled conclusions

  • Functional: production = vdW-DF2 (PBE+D2 anomalous — dropped).
  • Geometry: 60° staggered interface most stable (−42 meV, cell-fixed); H-passivated, length-converged scattering region.
  • Molecule is the conduction bridge: $T(E_F)$ = 0.70 / 0.35 / 0 for pristine / heterojunction / disconnected (k = 100, η = 0.005 eV); transmission eigenchannel $\tau_0\approx1$.
  • Length-independent: $T(E_F)$ = 0.43 / 0.35 / 0.29 across three scattering-region lengths — essentially flat.
  • Molecular frontier: Hf₂Se₉ HOMO/LUMO quasi-degenerate, pinned at $E_F$ (gap ≈ 0.024 eV, vdW-DF2); the Hf₂Se₉ chains are metallic.
  • DFT+Σ correction applied (2026-07-01/02): occupied levels shift down, virtual up; the isolated-molecule gap opens to 0.75 eV and the molecular resonance moves off $E_F$, strongly suppressing $T(E_F)$.
  • Corrected Landauer I–V (symmetric window): the correction suppresses the low-bias current. With a symmetric window ($\mu_{L,R}=\pm eV/2$) the curve is odd, $I(-V)=-I(V)$; the symmetric junction shows no rectification at this zero-bias level (earlier asymmetric-window figure corrected 2026-07-05).
