Computational setup for the current iteration

DFT (SIESTA)

  • Functional: vdW-DF2 (XC.authors LMKLL) — production for chain and junction relaxation, bands, PDOS. PBE+D2 was anomalously unstable for the Hf₂Se₉ relaxation, so it was dropped (02. Hf₂Se₉ stability).
  • Basis / grid: MeshCutoff 500 Ry; SCF.DM.Tolerance 1.0×10⁻⁷; density mixer weight 0.2, history 15.

Transport (TranSIESTA / TBtrans)

  • ~220-atom device (scattering atoms 13–208): VSe₃ buffer + Hf₂Se₉ + VSe₃ buffer between bulk VSe₃ electrodes.
  • Zero-bias NEGF; electrode k = 100 along transport; broadening η = 0.005 eV. Transmission $T(E)$ via sisl from trans.TBT.nc; eigenchannels (Re/Im/|ψ|² cubes + z-profile).

Molecular-level correction (DFT+Σ)

The GGA/vdW-DF level underestimates the molecular gap, mis-placing the resonance at $E_F$. Orbital shifts from the vacuum molecule, $\Delta_i=\varepsilon_i^{\text{B3LYP}}-\varepsilon_i^{\text{PBE}}$ (PBE in SIESTA vs B3LYP in VASP, vacuum-level aligned), are applied to the device Hamiltonian as a projector on the Hf/Se block,

$\displaystyle (\Delta H)_{\mu\nu}=\sum_i \Delta_i\,(C^i_\mu)^{*}C^i_\nu, \qquad (\mu,\nu)\in\text{Hf}_2\text{Se}_9,$

implemented through TBtrans's built-in δH (a deltaH.dH.nc file) with a ±1–2 eV energy window — no TranSIESTA source patch.
