Computational setup for the current iteration

DFT (SIESTA)

The production functional is vdW-DF2 (XC.authors LMKLL) for chain and junction relaxation, bands, and PDOS — PBE+D2 was anomalously unstable for the Hf₂Se₉ relaxation, so it was dropped (02. Hf₂Se₉ stability). Numerical settings: MeshCutoff 500 Ry, SCF.DM.Tolerance 1.0×10⁻⁸, density mixer weight 0.2 with history 15.

Transport (TranSIESTA / TBtrans)

The ~220-atom device (scattering atoms 13–208) places a Hf₂Se₉ molecule between VSe₃ buffers and bulk VSe₃ electrodes. Zero-bias NEGF uses electrode k = 100 along the transport direction and broadening η = 0.005 eV; the transmission $T(E)$ is extracted with sisl from trans.TBT.nc, together with the transmission eigenchannels (Re/Im/|ψ|² cubes).

Molecular-level correction (DFT+Σ)

The GGA/vdW-DF level underestimates the molecular gap, mis-placing the resonance at $E_F$. Orbital shifts from the vacuum molecule, $\Delta_i=[\varepsilon^{\text{B3LYP}}-\varepsilon^{\text{PBE}}]_{\text{VASP}} +[\varepsilon^{\text{PBE}}-\varepsilon^{\text{vdW-DF2}}]_{\text{SIESTA}}$ (same-code differences through a common PBE reference, so the inter-code offset cancels), are applied to the device Hamiltonian as a projector on the Hf₂Se₉ block,

$\displaystyle \Delta H = S\,C\,\mathrm{diag}(\Delta_i)\,C^{\dagger}\,S, \qquad (\mu,\nu)\in\text{Hf}_2\text{Se}_9,$

implemented through TBtrans's built-in δH (a deltaH.dH.nc file) with a $E_F\pm2$ eV energy window — no TranSIESTA source patch.
